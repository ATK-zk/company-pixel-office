# Music credits

Background music is in the spirit of a Ragnarok Online "Prontera"-style medieval capital town theme.
The original Prontera BGM (Gravity / SoundTeMP) is copyrighted, so it is **not** included.
Instead these public-domain (CC0) medieval town loops are used:

| File | Title / Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| assets/music/shakkar.(ogg,mp3) | "Shakkar" — Chris Murphy (zesona). Loop for a medieval game's main city. | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/shakkar |
| assets/music/village-square.(ogg,mp3) | "Medieval 5" (medievalvillagesquare) — Tozan. Village square BGM. | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-5 |

MP3 versions were transcoded from the original OGG files. Starts muted (browser autoplay rules); press 🔇 to play.
